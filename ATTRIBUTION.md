# Formal Attributions & Source Disclaimers

The **JP Professional Vocabulary Database** utilizes public government taxonomies, legal terminology lists, and professional association vocabularies for language-learning and machine-assisted educational curriculum generation. In accordance with Japanese Government Public Data License (PDL-1.0) and international fair use standards, the following explicit attributions apply:

---

## 1. 金融庁 (Financial Services Agency of Japan - FSA)

- **Official Source:** EDINET (Electronic Disclosure for Investors' NETwork) Taxonomy 2026年版 (Official Final Release) and 2027年版（案）.
- **Legal Notice:**
  > "© Copyright 2014 Financial Services Agency, The Japanese Government"
  > EDINET Taxonomy is developed and owned by Financial Services Agency, The Japanese Government.
- **Application in Database:**
  Accounting, bookkeeping, audit, and financial statement line items (勘定科目リスト, タクソノミ要素リスト, 国際会計基準タクソノミ要素リスト) are derived from the official EDINET Taxonomy files published by the FSA. The data has been normalized, structured, and enriched with linguistic pedagogical attributes (readings, Vietnamese translations, workplace collocations, examples, and TTS metadata) for non-commercial and educational language learning.

---

## 2. 国税庁 (National Tax Agency of Japan - NTA)

- **Official Source:** 国税庁 タックスアンサー (Tax Answer) indexes, 専門用語集 (Specialized Glossary), and statutory tax code indexes.
- **Application in Database:**
  Tax terminology, classification hierarchies (Income Tax 所得税, Corporate Tax 法人税, Consumption Tax 消費税, Withholding Tax 源泉徴収, Invoice System インボイス制度, Electronic Bookkeeping Act 電子帳簿保存法) are structured based on official guidelines from the National Tax Agency. Original explanatory text remains with the NTA; all learner explanations, Vietnamese meanings, and workplace dialogues are newly engineered original works.

---

## 3. 日本公認会計士協会 (JICPA)

- **Official Source:** 会計・監査用語かんたん解説集 (Glossary of Accounting & Auditing Terms).
- **Application in Database:**
  Utilized strictly as an authoritative terminology reference index to identify core audit and professional governance terms (e.g., 監査意見, 会計上の見積り, 継続企業の前提, 内部統制). JICPA's proprietary explanatory texts are not duplicated; all learner definitions are authored independently.
