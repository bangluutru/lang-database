# Source Policy & Data Governance Specification (Phase 1.1)

## 1. Overview & Core Principles

The **JP Professional Vocabulary Database** is built as an authoritative, traceable, and machine-queryable linguistic knowledge asset. Ingesting data requires strict adherence to legal compliance, intellectual property rights, data provenance, and automated auditability.

Under no circumstances is the Internet treated as "free raw data." Every single source ingested or referenced must pass through our **Source Governance Pipeline** before any term enters the candidate pool.

---

## 2. Origin Classification & Provenance Taxonomy

To eliminate ambiguity between direct extractions and expert curation, every entry carries an immutable `origin_type` in its `lineage` and `provenance` metadata:

| Origin Type | Definition | Criteria | Lineage Linking |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `official_extracted` | Verbatim terms extracted directly from official government / statutory registries. | Must exist in Layer B extracted datasets (`data/extracted/*.jsonl`) with verified `source_file` and `source_record_id`. | Links `extracted_candidate_id` and `normalized_candidate_id`. |
| `official_derived` | Statutory schedules, schedule lines, or standardized sub-elements derived from official statutes. | Standardized derived forms (e.g. `別表四`, `別表五`, statutory tax withholding brackets). | Links statutory legal reference. |
| `curated` | Standard professional workplace concepts and foundational vocabulary from expert lexical knowledge banks. | Practical business/accounting terms not explicitly listed in statutory title lists (e.g. `仕入`, `売上`, `報連相`). | Must declare `origin_type: curated` and must **never** masquerade as official extractions. |
| `generated_enrichment` | Learner explanations, Vietnamese translations, natural workplace examples, and dialogue. | Generated by linguistic engineering pipeline. | Never attributed to external regulatory authorities. |

---

## 3. Redistributable Sources vs. Validation References

In compliance with copyright safety (Section 21 of Directive):

1. **Redistributable Source (`redistributable_source`):**
   - Official Japanese Government open data (FSA, NTA, Japan Customs, JETRO) released under PDL-1.0 or open public terms of use.
   - Canonical term surfaces, official codes, English standard labels, and structural hierarchies may be parsed and redistributable.
2. **Validation Reference (`validation_reference`):**
   - General morphological dictionaries (Janome IPAdic) and legal lexicons used solely to verify readings and part-of-speech during validation.
   - Used **only** as reference ground-truth for pass/fail validation.
   - Long-form proprietary definitions or copyrighted dictionary text are **strictly prohibited** from ingestion or redistribution.

## 5. Annual Taxonomies & Draft Quarantine Policy

1. **Production Baseline:**
   - The production database is anchored strictly to **2026年版EDINETタクソノミ (Official Final Version)** published November 11, 2025.
2. **Draft Quarantine:**
   - Any draft version (such as **2027年版EDINETタクソノミ（案）**) is permanently quarantined in:
     `staging/fsa_edinet_2027_draft/`
   - Candidates originating from drafts are blocked by the independent validation pipeline (`validate_draft_contamination`) and can never pass the Release Gate into `data/production/`.

---

## 6. Anti-Bot and Scraping Ethics

1. **No Circumvention:** We never bypass CAPTCHA, bot detection, or scrape rate limits.
2. **Structured-First:** Direct downloadable structured formats (Excel `.xlsx`, ZIP archives, XML) are prioritized over web page scraping.
3. **Respectful Requests:** All web fetch operations must include explicit headers, rate limiting (minimum 1.0s delay between requests), and standard browser User-Agents.
4. **Reproducibility:** All downloads are mediated by scripted tools in `scripts/download_sources.py` which compute and log cryptographic checksums (SHA-256).
